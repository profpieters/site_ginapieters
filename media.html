Media

This page collects my work with the media: radio and podcast interviews, press quotes, opinion pieces, and coverage of my research, including appearances on call-in and live television shows.

For interviews, email me at gcpieters@de-rc.com. I keep standard hours in Chicago (US Central Time). If a story falls outside my expertise, I will say so and recommend someone better placed. Pieters is pronounced "Peters."

I consider public communication and education about economics, and about my research, an important part of my role. To protect my time, I no longer speak at events that do not cover my travel and accommodation, and if attendees must buy tickets, I ask for an honorarium. Standard exemptions for academia and policy institutions apply.
