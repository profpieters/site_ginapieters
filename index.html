Gina C. Pieters, Ph.D.

Economist working on digital money and financial markets. Referee, editor, and teacher of economics.

I research digital money and assets, quantitative investment, economics, and economics education; my papers and reports are collected here by field. I also speak to a wider audience on these topics, in radio and podcast interviews, press quotes, and opinion pieces. I have taught economics and finance courses from principles to the Ph.D. level at the University of Chicago, Trinity University, the University of Minnesota, and Collegio Internazionale Ca' Foscari, Venice.

My CV is available in both a short and detailed version. I am available for hire through my consulting firm, Digital Economy Research and Consulting.
