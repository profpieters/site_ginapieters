Cryptoasset Markets and/or Blockchain-enabled Decentralization

  1. “Bitcoin Reveals Exchange Rate Manipulation and Detects Capital Controls.” Resting. Media coverage: The Telegraph, Bitcoin News, Finance Magnates, Breaker Mag.
    Summary: Shows that bitcoin prices can approximate unofficial exchange rates and, once adjusted for bitcoin-market-specific elements, detect both the existence and the size of capital controls and exchange-rate manipulation, including transitory interventions that low-frequency data miss.
    Skills: Research computing: Stata. Data: constructing bitcoin exchange rates from daily transaction-weighted prices and official rates, for 17 currencies on four exchanges; benchmarking against KAOPEN capital controls and IMF regime classifications. Econometric: removing trends with a Lowess smoother; testing for unit roots (ADF, KPSS) with SBIC lag selection and LM autocorrelation tests; testing cointegration (Johansen trace, Pesaran–Shin–Smith bounds) on vector and conditional error-correction models; testing Toda–Yamamoto Granger causality in a VAR.
  2. “Are Cryptocurrency Markets Price Efficient? Depends on the Data Source.” Resting.
    Summary: Tests bitcoin price efficiency on the five largest exchanges and three price-aggregation sites and finds that the answer depends on the source: some series reject efficiency and others do not, and the disagreement persists as liquidity improves, which limits how far any empirical result generalizes.
    Skills: Theoretical economics: applying Fama’s (1970) definition of an efficient market. Mathematical: decomposing an aggregated price in fragmented markets. Research computing: Stata. Data: comparing price indexes with prices from the largest exchanges; selecting exchanges by trade volumes; computing log returns. Econometric: testing efficiency with Jarque–Bera, Box–Pierce Q, runs, and variance-ratio tests.

Quantitative Investment Research

  1. “Robust Search: Exploiting Tolerance To Optimize Trading Strategies.” Working paper, in progress.
    Summary: Presents a parameter sweep for trading strategies that searches only among robust settings: settings that keep passing when every parameter moves at once within a declared tolerance. On a synthetic surface with two robust regions, its first two phases certify both in every run; at six parameters, none of twelve alternatives given the same budget does so in any run.
